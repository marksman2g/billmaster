# Plaid Security Questionnaire Worksheet

Use this worksheet to gather factual answers before submitting Plaid's questionnaire. Do not copy secrets, bank credentials, full card numbers, or other sensitive values into this file or into chat.

| # | Plaid topic | Verified answer / evidence to gather |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Information-security contact | Name, title, and monitored email or group mailbox. |
| 2 | Documented security policy | Whether a written policy exists and is operationalized; note the policy location and review date. |
| 3 | Production access controls | The controls actually used, such as least privilege, role-based access, access reviews, or separate production accounts. |
| 4 | Consumer MFA before Plaid Link | Whether BillMaster requires consumer MFA before opening Plaid Link. |
| 5 | Critical-system MFA | Whether MFA protects systems that store or process consumer financial data. |
| 6 | TLS in transit | The deployed HTTPS/TLS configuration used between clients and servers. |
| 7 | Encryption at rest | The storage encryption controls applied to Plaid data at rest. |
| 8 | Vulnerability management | The scans, patching cadence, and remediation process actually in place for machines and production assets. |
| 9 | Privacy policy | The public privacy-policy URL for the BillMaster application, if published. |
| 10 | Consumer consent | Where and how users consent to collection, processing, and storage of their data. |
| 11 | Retention and deletion | The enforced retention/deletion rule, legal basis, and review cadence. |

If a control is not in place, record that honestly and document the remediation plan in Plaid's form. Do not select an answer just to advance the onboarding flow.
